Infrastructure & Transportation Summary — 2024
Executive Summary
Infrastructure upgrades in 2024 focused on road resurfacing, bicycle infrastructure, and public transit
improvements. This report covers completed and ongoing projects across all city districts for the
calendar year 2024 (January 1 - December 31, 2024). Total infrastructure investment in 2024 reached
$18.5M, representing a growth rate of +3.9% compared to 2023 ($17.8M) and continuing a 4-year
expansion trend with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of +11.8% since 2020.
